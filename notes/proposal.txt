CAR MAINTENANCE ASSISTANT Project Proposal

OVERVIEW

A simple web application that helps car owners manage vehicle
maintenance records, calculate upcoming service mileage, and understand
basic car maintenance through an AI text assistant.

The project will use Python, FastAPI, MongoDB, and a simple frontend.

PROBLEM & SOLUTION

Problem:

Car owners may: 
- Forget when their last maintenance was done 
- Forget when the next service is due 
- Have difficulty understanding basic car maintenance or components

Solution:

The Car Maintenance Assistant keeps maintenance information in one
place, calculates when the next service is due, and provides simple AI
explanations for car-related questions.

MAIN FEATURES

1.  Vehicle Management Users can add and manage vehicle information such
    as:

-   Brand & Model
-   Year
-   Current Mileage
-   Vehicle Type

2.  Maintenance Records Users can record and view maintenance such as:

-   Engine Oil
-   Tyres
-   Coolant
-   Brake Fluid
-   Air Filter

3.  Maintenance Calculator The system calculates the next maintenance
    mileage.

Example: Last Service: 110,000 km Service Interval: 10,000 km Current
Mileage: 118,500 km

Next Service: 120,000 km Due in: 1,500 km

4.  AI Text Assistant Users can ask simple car-related questions.(optional)

Example: User: What does engine coolant do? AI: Engine coolant helps
regulate engine temperature and prevent overheating.

PROJECT FLOW

Add Vehicle -> Record Maintenance -> Check Next Service -> Ask AI(optional)
